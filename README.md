# Delayed Reaction

**Your group chat, on your game clock.**

Watch the match an hour late and still watch it with your friends. Every reaction is pinned to the
*game clock*, so it lands on your screen at the moment it happened in the game, and never before.

Built for Fire TV (Vega OS), with a phone companion that works with any screen.

> Status: in active development for the Amazon *Build, Ship, Shape* hackathon (Fire TV track).
